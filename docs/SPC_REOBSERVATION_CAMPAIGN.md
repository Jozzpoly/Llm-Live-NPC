# SPC Next — Re-observation Campaign

Date: **2026-09-16**  
Status: **ACTIVE METHODOLOGY / STOP FEATURE MOMENTUM WHEN EXPERIENCE IS UNKNOWN**

This campaign exists because SPC repeatedly demonstrated a dangerous pattern: strong plans, correct local contracts and green automated evidence could coexist with a product that was visibly dead, misleading, regressed or far from the Owner's intended living-SPC experience.

The response is not to discard the project. The current World, continuity, epistemic, authority and deterministic foundations contain valuable recovered knowledge. The response is to stop treating proxy correctness as experienced truth and to re-observe the project through every available evidence plane before the next long build campaign.

## 1. Working diagnosis

The central problem is a **contract-to-oracle gap**.

Owner intent may be correct. Architecture documents may preserve that intent. Implementation may satisfy many local invariants. Yet the executable promotion gate can still measure a weaker proxy than the intended property.

Examples:

- `PNG exists and is non-empty` does not mean the depicted behavior is visually meaningful;
- deterministic replay can reproduce bad behavior perfectly;
- a canonical snapshot can prove causality while the participant-visible scene remains unreadable;
- research telemetry can explain a transition that the world itself does not express;
- a spectator camera without telemetry is not automatically a participant-bounded view;
- one resident's bounded slice can pass while the five-resident world remains lifeless;
- a fixture can be epistemically valid for the resident under test while visibly contaminating the product world;
- an isolated subsystem can preserve its contract while the end-to-end causal join required by a larger claim is absent.

Therefore a green gate qualifies only the exact property its oracle actually observes.

## 3. Re-observation unit

The campaign proceeds **experience by experience, not file by file**.

For every important resident/world behavior answer all of these:

1. What did we previously claim?
2. Which exact oracle supported that claim?
3. What does authoritative World truth say?
4. What does the involved resident privately know/perceive?
5. What does a detached spectator/research observer see?
6. What can the actual participant causally perceive?
7. What happens over time, not only at one checkpoint?
8. What happens after perturbation or interruption?
9. What happens after the nominal success event — does the aftermath remain coherent?
10. Did the test exercise the real product path or a fixture/API shortcut?
11. Can the same green oracle coexist with behavior that is obviously bad?
12. Which exact observation would falsify the current claim?

If these questions are not answered, the behavior remains only partially known.

## 4. Evidence-plane vocabulary

Use these labels literally.

### CANONICAL / CAUSAL

Authoritative World, resident-private state and execution/semantic authority have the intended causal relationship.

### COMPOSITION

The claim survives the actual runtime composition rather than isolated class tests or a synthetic shortcut.

### SPECTATOR-OBSERVED

A detached omniscient camera observed the physical behavior. This may reveal embodiment/readability defects but does not establish participant knowledge.

### PARTICIPANT-OBSERVED

The real Owner/player presentation observed the behavior under participant-bounded perception and camera rules.

### RESIDENT-PRIVATE

The selected resident acquired only causally legal information and its private state/history supports the behavior.

### RESEARCH-EXPLAINED

Detached telemetry/provenance can explain the causal transition without becoming authority.

### LIVE-PROVIDER

A real provider/model participated through the measured runtime path and survived timing/staleness/failure constraints.

### FIVE-RESIDENT PRESSURE

The property survives several independent resident lives/events/cognition simultaneously rather than one resident plus passive fixtures.

### OWNER-OBSERVED

Jozz personally exercised or observed the claimed behavior.

No plane silently promotes another.

## 5. Confidence classes for existing project behavior

During re-observation classify each behavior temporarily:

- **A — directly known:** causal + temporal behavior is observed through the relevant real experience plane and survives perturbation;
- **B — logically strong, experientially unobserved:** good tests/contracts exist but behavior has not yet been genuinely watched/used;
- **C — visually plausible, causally weak:** it looks right but truth/provenance is insufficient;
- **D — module/fixture evidence only:** capability exists mainly as isolated code/test composition;
- **E — observation instrument suspect:** view, camera, projection or harness may misrepresent the property.

Most existing work should be allowed to fall back to B/D/E without treating that as deletion or failure of all underlying engineering.

## 6. Hard rules for new sensory evidence

1. **Name the viewpoint.** Never write `participant` when the artifact is actually spectator/research.
2. **Artifact existence is instrument evidence only.** Pixel size/hash/non-mutation does not qualify the depicted behavior.
3. **Review temporal behavior.** Important motion/reaction/search/social behavior needs a sequence, trace or interactive dwell, not one endpoint screenshot.
4. **Inspect before fixing.** Preserve a failing/bad specimen long enough to understand what the player and resident actually experienced.
5. **Trace first divergence.** On mismatch, locate the first point where intended living behavior and observed behavior diverge.
6. **Fixture actors must not masquerade as product identities.** Research perturbations require explicit semantics and presentation isolation.
7. **Research UI cannot carry the life.** If the causal story exists only in text panels, world readability remains open.
8. **Aftermath is part of the test.** A successful action/message/interruption is not qualified until subsequent behavior remains coherent.
9. **Free dwell complements scripted gates.** Once bounded scenarios work, run the world long enough for unplanned composition defects to appear.
10. **Green is scoped evidence, never a verdict on the product.**

## 7. Campaign order

### ROBS-0 — calibrate our eyes

Before more resident capability growth:

- audit current presentation modes and label spectator vs participant truth correctly;
- eliminate or explicitly isolate fixture presentation contamination;
- establish a minimal real participant-bounded view;
- verify that screenshot/video capture itself is observationally non-interfering;
- preserve canonical/private/research views as separate lenses.

### ROBS-1 — replay the simplest foundations

Re-observe:

- five actor identities and body motion;
- facing vs velocity;
- region transitions;
- sight/hearing boundaries;
- player movement, camera, zoom and interaction;
- ordinary idle time;
- authored world/material readability.

Do not assume prior unit/browser PASS means these are experientially qualified.

### ROBS-2 — Janek material life

Replay the whole causal arc in increasing complexity:

`ordinary delivery -> stale knowledge -> local inspection -> semantic search -> legal reacquisition -> pickup -> interruption -> return -> aftermath`.

For every stage compare canonical truth, Janek-private evidence, spectator behavior and real participant presentation.

### ROBS-3 — Ida social life

Replay:

`accepted commitment -> remembered contact -> travel -> physical reacquisition -> addressed speech -> bystander hearing -> terminal consequence -> aftermath`.

Resolve the terminal causal-history ownership question instead of weakening the browser assertion.

### ROBS-4 — five residents at once

Stop treating the world as isolated resident demos. Run all five with independent matters/routines and observe for meaningful wall-clock/World time.

Follow one resident, switch to another, interfere, leave, return, move material state, speak, and allow unrelated residents to continue.

On anomaly:

`freeze World -> canonical truth -> relevant resident-private state -> causal provenance -> participant/spectator frame -> resume`.

### ROBS-5 — Owner living-world gate

Only after the assistant can repeatedly observe and manipulate the product through the relevant real views should an Owner-facing build be promoted for broad Jozz evaluation.

## 8. Long-term methodological change

The desired development loop is now:

`intent`
→ `bounded implementation`
→ `canonical causal qualification`
→ `machine observation of real behavior`
→ `participant-bounded observation`
→ `adversarial manipulation`
→ `aftermath observation`
→ `cross-check with private/research truth`
→ `correction`
→ `repeat`.

The Observatory is not a separate product. It is the sensory apparatus that allows Browser GPT to participate in this loop.

Its growth must be evidence-driven: add a new sense only when an important property cannot currently be observed or falsified.

## 9. Stop condition for this campaign

Re-observation is not finished because all old tests are green.

It is finished enough to resume a long capability-build campaign when:

- the main presentation/evidence views have explicit and truthful semantics;
- the simplest foundations have been re-exercised through actual runtime behavior;
- Janek and Ida slices are both causally and experientially understood rather than merely passing;
- at least one sustained five-resident session has been actively observed and perturbed;
- major mismatches between debug explanation and physical behavior are either corrected or explicitly recorded as blocking debt;
- we can state with evidence not only **what the architecture says should happen**, but **what actually happens when the world runs**.

The project remains a research furnace. Valuable failures, regressions and prior architecture are fuel; none of them earn immunity from being looked at again.
