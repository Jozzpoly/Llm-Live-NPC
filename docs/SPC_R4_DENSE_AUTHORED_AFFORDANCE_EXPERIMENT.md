## 1. R4 question

R1 proved that one resident can continue local embodied life without provider cognition manufacturing continuity.

R2 established a semantic-metabolism boundary so ordinary perception does not automatically become cognition pressure.

R3 established the first causal-personhood milestone: private resident history can change significance and provide grounded support for a legitimate competing-matter choice.

R4 now attacks a different bottleneck:

> **Does the authored World contain enough dense causal affordance for those resident systems to have something worth doing, noticing, failing at, recovering from and continuing — without travel/communication or provider novelty becoming the default outlet?**

This is not a content-volume campaign.

It is an experiment in **causal affordance density**.

## 2. Grounded baseline failure

The normal five-resident living-region specimen currently has:

- World bounds: `8192 × 8192`;
- 8 authored regions;
- 7 authored anchors;
- 5 residents plus the player;
- 1 material object: `crate.workshop.01`;
- World-owned material actions limited to the currently earned pickup/place surface.

Its own executable characterization already records:

> all five initial authored activities collapse to `idle` within 900 ticks / 15 seconds.

The baseline therefore demonstrates geographical distribution and substrate stability, not dense resident life.

The anchors currently provide authored spatial semantics and destinations. They are not, by themselves, rich stateful World affordances.

This is exactly the post-stress failure pattern:

> large space != living density.

## 4. Working definition: causal affordance density

Do **not** measure R4 by object count.

For this campaign, an authored fact contributes useful affordance density when several of these are true:

- it can be perceived or missed under real geometry;
- resident knowledge can differ from current World truth;
- it can enable or disable a local competence;
- it can participate in more than one resident matter or situation;
- it can change through resident or other-actor action;
- its changed state can alter later resident relevance;
- it can create a local alternative when another route is blocked;
- it can generate a factual consequence without semantic provider authority;
- it can matter differently to different residents because of their histories;
- it remains useful outside one scripted scenario.

A prop that only exists so one test can walk to it once contributes little density.

A simple crate may contribute much more if it can be:
- known;
- absent;
- carried;
- placed;
- moved by somebody else;
- searched for;
- encountered unexpectedly;
- relevant to different matters;
- visible to one resident but not another.

The unit is **causal reuse**, not content volume.

## 5. First pressure specimen: the dense workshop

R4 begins in a compact authored micro-world, not the 8192×8192 baseline.

The exact art/content is replaceable.

The first specimen should create at least three distinct local truth states from a small number of reusable material facts:

1. **available / locally executable**
   - one resident-owned matter has an object or condition the resident can handle with existing local competence;

2. **blocked by resident-private stale/absence evidence**
   - another legitimate matter concerns a recognized object that is not at the resident's remembered location;
   - checked absence is earned locally;
   - the matter remains unresolved;
   - no provider is required merely because one route is blocked;

3. **irrelevant or already-satisfied local truth**
   - at least one nearby authored fact exists but does not manufacture a new matter, action or cognition demand merely because it is present.

The key pressure is not the exact workshop fiction.

It is this:

> **One blocked local matter must not make the resident dead or force semantic-provider novelty when another grounded local possibility still exists.**

A later factual World change may make the blocked matter locally relevant again.

That reactivation must arise from new resident-acquired evidence, not omniscient polling.

## 6. Candidate life arc

The first experiment should naturally support something equivalent to:

1. resident begins with at least two legitimate continuing local matters or concerns;
2. both refer to real authored World facts;
3. one matter is locally executable;
4. the other reaches a truthful checked-absence / blocked state;
5. blocked state remains resident-owned and unresolved;
6. resident does **not** invent random travel/chatter and does **not** require provider cognition to prove life;
7. another legitimate local matter can continue through existing competence;
8. resident causes at least one factual material World change;
9. the blocked matter does not disappear or recursively manufacture semantic requests;
10. an external or other-actor World change later brings new evidence relevant to the blocked matter;
11. resident-private observation, not hidden World lookup, detects that change;
12. R2/R3 relevance may legitimately reactivate the matter;
13. resident can continue/resolve locally if the required competence is now unambiguous;
14. when no grounded local demand remains, stillness is again legitimate.

The exact sequence must emerge from state and authority, not one cinematic phase script.

## 7. Critical experimental fork: local competence vs semantic escalation

R4 must pressure the boundary between:

### Locally obvious method

Examples:

- the known visible basket should be returned to its authored place;
- a resident already carrying an object can continue to the known destination;
- a previously missing recognized object becomes visibly reacquired and the existing matter already says what factual action remains.

If meaning and method are already grounded, invoking a provider merely to say “pick it up” is a failure.

### Genuine semantic ambiguity

Examples:

- checked absence leaves several materially different search areas and no existing matter/history grounds one;
- an object is found but its changed context makes the previous matter questionable;
- two materially meaningful commitments conflict without a locally grounded reason to choose.

Those may remain R5-facing semantic questions.

R4 should increase the amount of life handled on the first side without cheating by hard-coding arbitrary preferences.

---

## 8. Anti-cheat rules

R4 FAIL if apparent density comes mainly from:

- increasing map size;
- arbitrary object-count targets;
- decorative anchors with no causal role;
- five bespoke routines for five object types;
- a scenario controller that says “now do B” after A;
- periodic task generation;
- random destination selection;
- random chores;
- provider-generated novelty;
- resident-ID branches;
- hidden World lookup presented as resident search;
- global knowledge of object relocation;
- an all-purpose affordance/planner framework introduced before the specimen demands it;
- every blocked local matter escalating immediately to an LLM;
- every visible object creating pressure or a matter;
- adding crafting/economy/inventory merely to create activity.

---

## 9. Reuse / invention policy

Prefer, in order:

1. compose existing World/material/knowledge/continuity mechanisms;
2. generalize a donor only when two real uses pressure the same missing seam;
3. add one minimal new World truth or local competence if the specimen cannot be expressed honestly;
4. introduce a broader framework only after repeated pressure proves it is cheaper and clearer than bounded mechanisms.

R4 must actively try to **avoid** a new generic affordance system.

If simple authored facts plus existing resident matters/knowledge/competence are enough, that is a better result.

- baseline stability is not equivalent to living affordance density;

## 12. R4-B — zero-provider dense-workshop specimen

Provider cognition remains disabled.

Minimum promotion pressure:

- compact space;
- more than one legitimate resident-local matter/opportunity;
- at least one executable and one blocked/stale opportunity;
- shared local competence reused rather than duplicated;
- at least one factual material consequence;
- blocked matter survives without dominating body life;
- no random work generation;
- no semantic heartbeat;
- at least one irrelevant nearby fact remains irrelevant.

A useful result would demonstrate:

> **affordance alternatives keep local life coherent when one path fails.**

## 15. Candidate metrics — research only

Do not freeze KPIs.

Useful observations include:

- number of grounded local opportunities visible/known to resident;
- fraction of nearby authored facts that remain correctly irrelevant;
- local factual consequences per provider request, with provider count expected to be zero in R4-B;
- duration of coherent local life while one matter is blocked;
- number of resident matters preserved through blocked/executable alternation;
- repeated action on already-satisfied state;
- hidden-truth leaks;
- number of bespoke code paths required per new affordance;
- causal reuse: how many different situations use the same World fact / competence;
- quiet interval after locally meaningful work ends.

A higher action rate is not automatically better.

## 16. R4 PASS direction

Do not pre-freeze a final architecture.

A future R4 PASS must at minimum defend:

- a compact authored surface materially denser than the current baseline;
- multiple grounded local possibilities using shared mechanisms;
- one blocked route does not force resident inactivity or provider novelty;
- factual World consequences occur through existing authority;
- resident-private knowledge remains honest;
- irrelevant affordances do not become automatic matters/pressure;
- changed World truth can later alter local relevance through legal perception;
- at least two resident pressure fixtures can use the shared substrate;
- no generic content/task churn is used to simulate life;
- R1/R2/R3 nonregression.

The deeper promotion question is:

> **Did a small increase in authored causal reality produce a disproportionately richer resident-local life surface?**

If not, adding more content is not the answer.
