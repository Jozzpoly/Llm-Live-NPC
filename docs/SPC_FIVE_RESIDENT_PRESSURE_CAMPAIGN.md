# SPC Next — Five-Resident Pressure Campaign

> **CURRENT EXECUTION NOTICE (2026-09-25): RESIDENT-PRESSURE / SCENARIO DONOR MAP.** Keep the pressure dimensions and anti-special-case principles here, but do not follow its historical immediate order as a roadmap. Current execution authority is `docs/SPC_POST_STRESS_RECOVERY_PROGRAM.md`; current R6 direction is `docs/SPC_R6_COMPLEMENTARY_PERSONHOOD_EXPERIMENT.md`.


Date: **2026-09-15**  
Status: **LONG-HORIZON RESEARCH PROGRAM / NOT A FEATURE ROADMAP**

This document turns the current five-resident specimen into a systematic research program for growing one shared resident architecture over weeks without attempting to build the entire final world at once.

The residents are **pressure fixtures, not special architectures**.

No core subsystem should contain logic equivalent to:

`if resident == Janek ...`

except authored identity/content initialization. Every capability earned through one resident must belong to the shared World/resident/local-brain architecture and be available to the others when their own situation requires it.

The role pressures are therefore not personalities and not scripts. They are different adversarial questions asked of one system.

---

# 1. Why five residents are useful now

The original five-resident specimen risked becoming five copies of the same resident with different starting positions and initial activities.

The recovery campaign can invert that weakness into a research advantage.

Each resident can pressure a different dimension of continuity:

- **Janek** — material life and interruption;
- **Ida** — social information and testimony;
- **Oren** — expedition, resource consequence and long-range continuity;
- **Nela** — exploration, ignorance and divergent world knowledge;
- **Mira** — ordinary settlement life, human contact and multi-matter continuity.

The goal is not to finish one resident and move to the next permanently. The sequence is an **expanding vertical spiral**: each campaign adds one difficult dimension, then earlier scenarios are rerun to catch regressions and cross-coupling.

---

# 2. Shared gates for every pressure campaign

Every resident scenario should eventually be judged across several independent evidence planes.

## Causal truth

World facts and resident authority are correct even without UI.

## Composition truth

World, resident evidence, matter authority, local brain, action outcomes and cognition compose through the actual runtime path rather than isolated tests.

## Observable truth

Relevant cause/effect is physically legible in the world without relying on explanatory debug text.

## Participant truth

A player learns only what the participant could causally perceive. Research omniscience is not silently helping the experience.

## Research truth

A detached causal witness can explain the state transition without becoming World or resident authority.

## Live-provider truth

Where semantic cognition matters, the real provider survives latency/failure/stale/abandon conditions without taking control of the body.

## Five-resident truth

The same capability survives unrelated concurrent residents/events and does not create shared-mind knowledge or global invalidation.

No single plane promotes the whole scenario.

# 3. Campaign J — Janek: material continuity + interruption

## Research question

Can one resident have a continuing material matter, execute it locally through factual World actions, be interrupted without losing itself or the matter, and continue/revise based on what actually happened?

## Strong first specimen

One stable logical crate:

`crate.workshop.01`

One authored origin near the workshop and one destination near the settlement/crossroads/market side.

The exact content is replaceable. The important part is a tangible object whose state can become false, unavailable, carried, moved and observed.

## Minimal World truths

Potential bounded model:

- stable object ID;
- position when free;
- `heldBy` / possession when carried;
- one actor holding capacity for this experiment;
- authoritative pickup/drop/place request and result;
- range/availability/placement validation;
- factual before/after outcome occurrence;
- physical/public rendering derived from current truth.

No inventory, economy, ownership law or generic item framework is required.

## Local brain pressure

Local execution should discover a minimal set of reusable skills under pressure, likely beginning with concepts such as:

- approach known entity/position;
- attempt pickup;
- carry toward destination;
- attempt placement/drop;
- wait/retry when transiently unavailable;
- check last-known location;
- report local method exhaustion as semantic pressure.

Do not let the LLM author a long capability-step program.

## Adversarial sequence

J0 — World object/action truth only.  
J1 — deterministic matter → run → pickup/carry/place with no LLM.  
J2 — player calls/interacts during execution; temporary attention/execution disturbance then local return.  
J3 — object moved by player while observed.  
J4 — object moved while unobserved; resident returns to stale last-known location and gains checked-absence evidence.  
J5 — local search/known alternatives exhaust; genuine semantic discrepancy appears.  
J6 — scoped semantic provider decides how the existing matter changes, under real latency/failure.  
J7 — other residents/noise/events coexist without erasing Janek's continuity.

## Questions deliberately left open

- Is one foreground run enough during conversation?
- Does matter need semantic suspension, or only run/channel hold?
- When is a social interruption a temporary interaction vs a new continuing matter?
- Does pickup/carry need separate task runs or one durative run?
- When does temporary unavailability become semantic unavailability?

The scenario should answer these experimentally.

# 4. Campaign I — Ida: social epistemics + message consequence

## Research question

Can information move through the world without becoming shared omniscience?

## Candidate pressure

Ida learns or is asked to carry one piece of information/message from one actor/place to another.

The information itself does not become World truth merely because somebody said it.

## Required distinctions

- heard statement vs believed proposition;
- recognized speaker vs anonymous voice;
- addressee vs bystander;
- direct witness vs testimony;
- factual occurrence vs semantic content of speech;
- source provenance through retelling;
- personal knowledge vs possible future group/institutional knowledge.

## Adversarial cases

- Mira tells Ida something false or outdated;
- Janek overhears but is not addressed;
- recipient cannot be found at last-known location;
- Ida meets the recipient through unrelated travel;
- another resident repeats a second-hand report;
- two residents hold conflicting beliefs grounded in different histories.

## Why this campaign matters

It should force resident-private social memory and evidence provenance without creating a universal dialogue/relationship framework prematurely.

Feniks social/epistemic causality is the north star; T0 is the first tiny recovered boundary.

# 5. Campaign O — Oren: expedition + resource + return

## Research question

Can a resident leave the dense local area, continue an own matter over a long route, interact with material/resource state, accumulate a private history, and return to a world that may have changed independently?

## Feniks donor

Expedition loop hypothesis:

`prepare`
→ `leave known place`
→ `gather information/risk`
→ `alter world`
→ `carry consequences`
→ `return`
→ `preserve meaningful history`
→ `depart later from changed conditions`.

## Near-term high-fidelity pressure

Initially run the whole expedition at normal fidelity. Do **not** implement offscreen LOD yet.

Pressure:

- long-running matter;
- knowledge-limited region navigation;
- route interruption/recovery;
- material acquisition;
- return with an object/fact/consequence;
- settlement changed during absence;
- Oren does not know changes he could not observe.

## Later temporal/LOD pressure

Only after the same expedition is causally qualified at full fidelity:

- demote routine unobserved continuation;
- preserve identity/matter/evidence freshness;
- promote on conflict/observation/significance;
- compare high-fidelity run vs coarse run for materially equivalent truth;
- never fabricate unsupported exact unseen drama.

This is a future Feniks-facing bridge, not current implementation scope.

# 6. Campaign N — Nela: exploration + divergent world model

## Research question

Can one resident genuinely know a different world than the others because she has physically acquired different evidence?

## Pressure areas

- unknown vs familiar vs visited places;
- physical discovery of authored places/objects;
- no hidden route/map leakage;
- remembered route/contact can become stale;
- deliberate search/look/inspection;
- checked absence;
- exploration history that later changes decisions;
- return and testimony to other residents without magically copying all knowledge.

## Adversarial cases

- Nela discovers something no one else has seen;
- she returns by a route others do not know;
- she reports the discovery incompletely or incorrectly;
- another resident later verifies/refutes it;
- authored world changes after Nela's earlier visit;
- navigation must distinguish remembered topology from current hidden World truth.

This campaign should force the project beyond `knownRegions` toward real private spatial/world knowledge only when needed.

# 7. Campaign M — Mira: ordinary life + human contact

## Research question

Can a resident feel alive when nothing dramatic is happening?

This may eventually be the hardest pressure campaign.

## Why Mira matters

Systems often look intelligent during explicit tasks because the task itself provides structure. A convincing inhabitant must also survive ordinary time:

- local routine;
- small self-generated transitions;
- incidental meetings;
- short conversation without abandoning own life;
- multiple low-level continuing matters;
- silence without becoming dead;
- delayed follow-up to earlier events;
- human presence that influences but does not become the center of the universe.

## Failure modes to attack

- waiting for the player as a service NPC;
- chatter used to simulate aliveness;
- random activity switching with no causal history;
- periodic LLM calls manufacturing arbitrary novelty;
- all resident priorities collapsing whenever the player speaks;
- invisible debug explanation carrying more life than the physical scene.

Mira should become the final test that the recovered architecture can **live**, not merely complete tasks.

# 8. Cross-resident campaigns

After individual pressure dimensions exist, they must collide.

Important future combinations:

## Janek × Ida

A material matter and a social message compete for attention/execution without erasing either history.

## Janek × player × Companion-style movement

A resident carrying a real object encounters physical contention. Hard safety, cooperation and matter urgency remain separate questions.

## Oren × settlement change

One actor is away while others materially change home conditions.

## Nela × Ida

Private exploration knowledge becomes testimony rather than shared truth.

## Mira × all

Routine settlement life continues while expeditions, work, messages and player interference occur nearby.

These are where five independent minds become more than five isolated demos.

# 10. Anti-special-case rule

A pressure resident may own authored facts/capabilities/history that others do not, but core logic must be shared.

Allowed differences:

- identity/name;
- authored background/familiarity;
- competences earned or assigned as world/life facts;
- current matters;
- private experiences/beliefs;
- physical state/capabilities;
- relationships/history.

Rejected shortcut:

- hard-coded behavior by resident ID;
- provider prompt paragraph standing in for causal capabilities;
- special World mutation path available only to the test resident;
- scripted outcome that bypasses the same authority another resident would require.

# Closing rule

The project should not ask:

> "Have we implemented memory / dialogue / navigation / LOD?"

It should increasingly ask:

> **"Can this resident live through this causal situation, remain the same resident afterward, and leave the world in a truthful changed state?"**

Five different pressure residents give us a practical way to ask that question repeatedly until the shared architecture deserves the word `living`.
