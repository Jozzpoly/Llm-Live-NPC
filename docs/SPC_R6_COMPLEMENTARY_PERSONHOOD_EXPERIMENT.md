## 1. Why R6 exists

The post-stress campaign has now defended the machinery that a living resident needs:

- zero-provider local life;
- private relevance / semantic-pressure lifecycle;
- first causal-personhood effect from resident-owned continuity/history;
- dense authored material affordances with legitimate non-action;
- sparse higher cognition with explicit origin and settlement;
- provider latency without freezing World life;
- stale/newer-attention rejection;
- decline/defer/clarify/error homeostasis;
- one real GPT-5.6 Luna escalation through the same local admission membrane;
- a literal hard experiment budget that blocked a second upstream request.

Those are foundations.

They are not the product goal.

The Owner goal remains five residents who are players of their own lives:
- own matters and goals;
- preferences and aversions;
- refusal and self-interest;
- relationships and obligations;
- habits and ordinary continuity;
- comfort, irritation and curiosity where causally earned;
- ownership/control/attachment where causally earned;
- ability to ignore the player;
- behavior that remains coherent when nobody is directing them.

R6 returns the campaign from provider mechanics to that problem.

## 4. R6 core hypothesis

> **Ordinary personhood can emerge from persistent private causal history that changes what matters to the resident and what commitments continue to exist, rather than from roleplay prose or repeated provider invention.**

A useful R6 personhood fact must have:

1. an inspectable origin;
2. bounded structured meaning;
3. private resident ownership;
4. lifecycle / revision / termination semantics;
5. at least one observable effect on significance, choice or durable commitment;
6. no direct body authority;
7. no resident-name special case.

## 6. R6-A — resident-originated standing social commitment

Status: **QUALIFIED / BOUNDED PASS — 2026-09-21**

Qualification record:
`docs/SPC_R6A_STANDING_SOCIAL_COMMITMENT_PASS.md`

R6-A first made the R5 future-promise gap executable, then introduced the smallest bounded private-state primitive justified by that failure.

The qualified causal chain is:

1. one exact addressed Ida speech creates one resident-owned semantic attempt;
2. a provider-shaped decision accepts one grounded `communicate` matter/run;
3. a standing-commitment capability can be prepared only while that exact communicate run is live;
4. provider completion and semantic preparation alone create no standing history;
5. Mira factually speaks the promised sentence through World authority;
6. the communicate run resolves from exact factual outcome provenance;
7. only then can the prepared capability materialize one private `standing_social_commitment`;
8. that matter owns no run and no body authority;
9. it persists while Ida physically leaves;
10. the same later Ida sight-enter becomes resident-relative semantic pressure because of that exact private history;
11. private release resolves the commitment and locally invalidates the derived pressure without manufacturing a World fact;
12. an equivalent later Ida sight-enter is again observation-only.

This qualifies one bounded causal-personhood effect beyond provider prose and beyond the terminal communicate obligation.

It does **not** qualify:
- full R6;
- general promise understanding;
- automatic inference of commitments from arbitrary speech text;
- preferences, aversions, trust, resentment, habit or self-interest;
- cross-resident behavioral genericity of this new primitive;
- ordinary Owner-observed aliveness.

Authored `role/drives` remain non-authoritative exactly as established by R3.

## 7. R6-B — native life-intent standing continuation

Status: **QUALIFIED / BOUNDED PASS — 2026-09-22**

Qualification record:
`docs/SPC_R6B_NATIVE_STANDING_SOCIAL_CONTINUATION_PASS.md`

R6-B removes the experiment-owned `prepare-standing` / `materialize-standing` knowledge from the normal causal path.

An accepted `communicate` life-intent may now explicitly declare only that the exact future communication is intended to continue as standing social responsibility:

`standingSocialCommitment = { goal }`

That declaration is resident-owned semantic intent after local admission, but it is still inert with respect to standing history and body authority.

The durable standing matter can arise only after:

1. one exact private origin reason survives normal life-intent admission;
2. one grounded `communicate_actor` matter/run exists;
3. the standing declaration was already part of that resident-owned communicate meaning;
4. the exact resident speech occurs factually in World;
5. the exact source run outcome reconciles successfully.

The durable commitment wording is **the exact factual communication text** carried by the source matter. There is no second provider-authored promise paraphrase that could disagree with what the resident actually said.

Text alone still does not imply a standing commitment. An ordinary communication containing future-sounding language but lacking the explicit continuation declaration remains ordinary communication and creates no standing history.

R6-B preserves the qualified R6-A lifecycle/significance effects:
- run-free persistence;
- no provider heartbeat;
- later actor-relative significance;
- private release;
- observation-only equivalent contact after release;
- no direct body authority.

R6-B is not full R6 and does not yet qualify cross-resident behavioral genericity.

## 7.1 Current frontier — cross-resident genericity

The implementation is resident-generic by construction, but that is not sufficient evidence.

The next bounded falsifier must run the same native causal chain through a second resident without using the Mira/R5 fixture or resident-name policy:

`different resident -> normal causal cognition admission -> factual self speech -> own standing history`.

This must prove:
- different resident id and identity namespace;
- different counterparty;
- exact private origin;
- normal resident-generic life-intent/cognition authority;
- no standing history before factual speech;
- durable commitment text exactly equals that resident's factual speech;
- run-free standing matter after source completion;
- no Mira-specific helper or policy.

Only after this can the broader first R6 milestone claim generic-across-residents support.

## 8. Anti-cheat rules

R6 FAILS if apparent personhood mainly comes from:

- adding personality adjectives to the prompt;
- adding more verbose `drives`;
- random action variation;
- per-resident hard-coded utility weights;
- a generic needs/Sims meter without a concrete failure;
- provider-authored “I like / hate / trust / promised” prose with no independent origin;
- treating one speech string as durable obligation without lifecycle;
- creating a matter for every emotion or preference;
- making personhood state directly seize body authority;
- measuring success by dialogue quality alone;
- treating “five different names” as five different people.

## 9. Evidence planes

Keep separate:

- **ORIGIN** — what exact event/history created the private personhood fact?
- **LIFECYCLE** — open, revised, fulfilled, released, superseded?
- **SIGNIFICANCE** — does identical current evidence matter differently because of history?
- **CHOICE** — does a legal ambiguity expose causally different support?
- **NON-ACTION / REFUSAL** — can the resident legitimately decline or ignore?
- **BODY AUTHORITY** — self state never moves/speaks by itself;
- **WORLD OUTCOME** — actual actions remain factual World consequences;
- **PROVIDER ROLE** — provider may judge conflict but may not invent why the resident cared;
- **COMPLEMENTARY JANek** — material competence/continuity does not regress.

## 10. Promotion direction

Do not freeze full R6 PASS criteria yet.

The first milestone should at minimum demonstrate one new ordinary-personhood fact beyond open-matter responsibility that:

- existed before the decision it influences;
- has exact private causal origin;
- persists across time without provider heartbeat;
- changes significance or a real legal choice;
- can terminate/change without deleting history;
- survives deterministic and browser evidence;
- is generic across residents.

Only then decide whether the next pressure should be:
- preference/aversion;
- relationship history;
- ownership/control;
- habit;
- personal inconvenience / self-interest;
- another Owner-relevant dimension.
