## 3. Anti-cheat boundary

The native contract deliberately does **not** infer a promise from language.

A future-sounding communicate text without `standingSocialCommitment` remains ordinary communication. The existing R6 future-social-meaning characterization stays green as the negative control.

The provider may declare only the bounded semantic fact that this communication is intended to continue as standing responsibility and supply its goal.

The provider may not provide an independent durable commitment wording. Therefore resident history cannot say X when the resident factually said Y.

The declaration:
- creates no standing history by itself;
- grants no body authority;
- proves no outcome;
- cannot bypass exact communicate grounding;
- cannot survive stale/invalid admission authority as a factual result.
