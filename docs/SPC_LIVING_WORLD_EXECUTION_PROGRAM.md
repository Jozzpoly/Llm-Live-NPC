## 1. Product truth

The project is building **five genuinely living residents in one shared causal authored world**.

Mira, Janek, Ida, Oren and Nela are not five chatbots, five scripted scenarios or five copies of one generic NPC. They are the first serious specimen of a future living RPG world in which:

- the player is one participant, not the center of reality;
- each resident has a private, incomplete and possibly stale perspective;
- residents keep their own matters, routines and unfinished consequences through time;
- local embodied intelligence continues between LLM calls;
- LLM cognition contributes judgement, reinterpretation, prioritisation and difficult semantic decisions;
- physical and social facts arise through World causality, not from provider narration;
- residents can affect one another and later disagree because they lived through different histories;
- the Owner can enter, interfere, leave, return and discover whether the inhabitants continued to exist coherently.

The long-term direction is the Owner's dream of a **living authored RPG / mini-world**, with the locality, traversal, return, danger, social causality and sense of place that motivated the Tibia-like / mini-MMO direction, without preserving legacy combat, quests, classes or old implementation constraints.

The governing shorthand remains:

> **meaning may be generative; reality must remain grounded.**

And the newer product correction is equally binding:

> **we are building residents; the World is their causal substrate, not the product we build instead of them.**

## 5. The Owner test is a development instrument, not a ceremony

The first Owner sandbox is not acceptance testing for a finished game.

It is the point where **human experience becomes the highest-value next sensor**.

The Owner should be free to:

- roam;
- watch residents without talking to them;
- leave an area and return;
- address one resident and then another;
- interrupt somebody who is doing something;
- observe who hears and who does not;
- follow a resident;
- interfere with movement or local situations;
- wait long enough to see whether life continues without direct prompting;
- judge whether actions have understandable physical/social causes;
- notice behavior that is technically valid but feels fake, mechanical, service-NPC-like or dead.

Do not force the Owner through a brittle scripted QA checklist unless a specific defect requires reproduction.

Owner qualitative feedback may invalidate a technically green implementation. That is expected.

## 6. Roadmap after the first Owner sandbox

This roadmap is **pressure ordered, not calendar ordered and not a feature checklist**.
Every phase must be re-planned using current Owner feedback and live evidence.

### Phase L1 — Owner-observed ordinary life

Primary question:

> Can five residents feel like inhabitants during ordinary time, not only while executing explicit tests?

Pressure:
- silence without inertness;
- small self-generated transitions with causal reasons;
- continuing low-level matters;
- interruption without player-centrality;
- delayed follow-up;
- meetings that happen because trajectories/lives intersect;
- legitimate stillness distinguished from dead runtime.

Mira is the strongest pressure resident for this phase because ordinary settlement life exposes fake aliveness better than explicit quests.

Rejected shortcut:
- random wandering;
- periodic chatter;
- periodic LLM novelty for its own sake;
- cosmetic status churn.

### Phase L2 — generic capability convergence

Primary question:

> Can capabilities already proven in vertical slices become shared resident competences without creating resident-specific scripts?

Likely pressures:
- generic investigate/search from private knowledge;
- checked absence;
- pickup/carry/place/material manipulation;
- interaction outcomes;
- richer communication/contact;
- interruption of material work and exact return.

Janek remains the strongest material-pressure resident.

Rule:
**promote donors into the unified runtime only when a live resident situation needs them.**
Do not migrate every old capability merely because it exists.

### Phase L3 — five independent lives collide

Primary question:

> Do we still have five minds when their concerns compete in the same place and time?

Pressure combinations:
- Janek material work × Ida social delivery;
- Nela private discovery × Ida testimony;
- Oren absence × settlement change;
- Mira ordinary routine × everybody else's traffic;
- player interruption while resident-to-resident matters are already active;
- two residents competing for body-space, objects, attention or timing.

Desired result:
not five scenario lanes running in parallel, but one world where independent lives create consequences for one another.

### Phase L4 — deepen the local live brain

Primary question:

> Which failures actually require richer continuous local intelligence rather than another LLM request?

Candidate capabilities are earned only by observed pressure:
- attention/facing;
- local spatial reasoning;
- collision/right-of-way;
- obstacle recovery;
- manipulation sequencing;
- procedural competence;
- short-horizon improvisation;
- routine continuation;
- local social/physical reactions.

Do not start by selecting BT / Utility AI / GOAP / HTN or another framework.
Use techniques as donors after the resident behavior demands them.

### Phase L5 — world richness earned by resident life

Primary question:

> What world structure is missing because residents currently have too little meaningful reality to live in?

Possible earned additions:
- richer material objects;
- work/resource loops;
- authored places with different opportunities/risks;
- traversal pressure;
- ownership/control facts;
- environmental consequences;
- simple ecology/economy only where resident situations require it.

World expansion must create **causal life pressure**, not content volume.

### Phase L6 — temporal depth, persistence and LOD

Only after ordinary live behavior is compelling at full fidelity:

- save/restore resident continuity;
- long absence and return;
- world changes while one resident is elsewhere;
- durable causal biography;
- offscreen/coarse simulation;
- LOD's World style fidelity changes driven by observability/significance.

Hard invariant:
LOD may change computational representation, never the laws or truth of the world.

This is intentionally late. Do not solve future scale before the full-fidelity resident is worth scaling.

## 8. Decision hierarchy

When multiple useful tasks compete, prefer in this order:

1. **Owner north-star / living-resident value**
2. **integrated resident behavior in the actual unified runtime**
3. **Owner-observable participant experience**
4. **causal/epistemic correctness**
5. **falsification power of the next experiment**
6. **shared capability/generalisation earned by repeated pressure**
7. infrastructure elegance
8. abstract completeness / feature count

A technically beautiful change that does not improve or protect resident life may be deferred indefinitely.

## 9. Self-correction triggers

Stop current momentum and re-plan if any of these become true:

- two or more implementation tranches pass without producing a stronger resident-life observation;
- the work is mostly about a framework, engine boundary or instrumentation and no current resident scenario demands it;
- a green test cannot be translated into a concrete statement about what a resident can now live through;
- a browser/debug panel contains more evidence of aliveness than the world itself;
- new behavior exists only because a resident ID or scenario name selects special logic;
- LLM responses are substituting for missing local competence or factual World outcomes;
- infrastructure work is justified mainly by hypothetical future scale;
- the player starts becoming the automatic priority of every resident;
- random novelty is being used to hide lack of continuing matters;
- a promotion claim relies on a weaker evidence plane than the claim itself.

When triggered:
**reconstruct the real Owner objective, inspect live evidence, identify the smallest resident-life pressure that exposes the problem, and continue from there.**

- private resident state;
- World truth;
- browser presentation;
